# Floor Fracture on Quest 3

**Target cut · 3 minutes 20 seconds.** Capture these short takes through the headset recorder. The separate Editor rehearsal shows the shot order. For takes that use a fixed seed or a selected landing, use the development build’s visible **Development** menu.

| In the finished cut | What to record on the headset |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:12 | Open on a cracking pane. Step onto intact glass before zero, hold the safe square and look through the opening as the fragments fall. Keep the occupied ring and the breaking pane together in view. |
| 0:12–0:40 | At Welcome, turn slowly from the glass perimeter to the stone around it. Frame a real furniture base, look left and right, then show the open space between chair legs. In a separate take on the idle board, move a small bin and show both its former and current positions. Keep the physical room in the recording so alignment is visible. |
| 0:40–1:03 | Choose **Tutorial**. Look at the ring and the crack underfoot, then take one step to neighboring clear glass. After the original pane returns, choose **Try the stone**, grab it, release it over an intact pane and let the pane reform. |
| 1:03–1:32 | Start a Classic wave and step away before zero. In Memory, frame the warning while it is visible, hold the shot as it disappears, then step to the place you remembered. End on the updated survival count or the next countdown. |
| 1:32–1:56 | Use **Development → Seed 379 · level 3**. Record a sliding pane beside ordinary glass. Catch it, hold the view as the ring settles, then show it crack and break in a later wave. Record this take when the fitted board offers a moving pane. |
| 1:56–2:29 | Select Water and record a deadline loss from an off-center part of a pane. Start looking through the pane. Briefly look back at the glass and brick rising together, then follow the shaft toward the water. Keep the splash, underwater view and fade together. |
| 2:29–2:52 | Select Space for another loss. Hold a downward view of the destination, then look ahead near impact so the landing and its change into the surrounding space read clearly. |
| 2:52–3:20 | Show **Settings → Fall motion · Reduced** and one shortened landing. In a second fall, make a fresh pinch or press A or X to skip. Finish on **Restart** with fresh intact glass. |

Keep game sound in the edit. If you enable **Settings → Narration**, leave the practice or Results prompt audible before speaking. Record the furniture take and the falling-arena take as wearer observations, and describe any residual edge movement exactly as it appears.
