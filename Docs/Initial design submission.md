# Floor Fracture initial design submission

The eight-page [design PDF](<./Floor Fracture Design Document.pdf>) is prepared for the November 20, 2026 deadline. Moodle submission is unconfirmed. Wais’s later five-page revision and the [editable final working draft](<./Floor Fracture Final Design Working Draft.docx>) contain all 11 sections of the [course template](<./Final Project - Design Doc.docx>). The page references below refer to the original eight-page PDF.

## Course requirements in the design

| Course requirement | Planned player experience or test | PDF page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Controller ray and direct interaction | Point either controller at a menu and press the trigger. Squeeze the grip to pick up the practice stone and release it onto a pane. Gravity carries it through the broken glass. | 3–5 |
| Room awareness and real-surface placement | Fit the board to the scanned floor with clearance around furniture. The player checks the outlined footprint in passthrough and can reject it if an obstacle is in the way. | 2–4 |
| Settings and feedback | Preview the warning intensity, adjust the volume, and choose a full or reduced-motion fall. A menu button lights up on hover and confirms selection with a click and a light controller pulse. | 5 |
| Spatial audio | Hear glass break where the stone lands, then hear a quieter echo from below the floor. The countdown is also audible while the player looks for a safe tile. | 4–5 |
| Comfort and accessibility | Use menus seated or standing. Move to safety with an ordinary step. A pulsing rim and branching cracks distinguish unsafe glass. The player can reduce motion or skip the fall. | 3, 5, 7 |
| Outside testing | Observe at least two outside players trying placement, the stone, a wave, a loss and a restart. Record their words, any change to the game, and what happens on a repeat attempt. Tester findings are pending. | 7–8 |
| Quest 3 performance | Use OVR Metrics Tool on the headset. The targets are at least 72 FPS and under 13.9 ms per frame. Frame rate, frame time, draw calls and triangle counts are pending physical measurements. | 5–6 |

Page 4 describes the first minute, page 5 lists the menu controls, and page 7 describes the fall and restart. Pages 7–8 cover playtesting, and page 8 lists the course deadlines.
